THE CAPABILITY PROGRAM

The Curriculum.

The guide builds your business's foundation. This builds you. Five levels of skill, from your first serious prompt to work at the edge of the field, with a track for individuals and a track for companies at every step. Free, open, MIT licensed. Nothing is gated.

Two programs. Know which one you are holding.

Groundwork is one system with two halves, and they run on different axes. The doctrine has six layers: things you build once for your business, in order. The Curriculum has five levels: stages of skill you climb yourself, at your own pace. The numbers never map one to one, because they are not counting the same thing.

Everything Groundwork publishes is one of three documents. Here is each one, what it is for, and how to get it. All three are free.

DocumentWhat it isHow to get it
The Guide The doctrine on the web: six layers, three phases, fifteen documents, one Operator. What you build for your business, with every template. Read it on this site
The Field Guide The same doctrine as a 29-page printable PDF. It is generated from the guide pages, so the two can never disagree. Download the PDF
The Curriculum The skill path: five levels, two tracks, from first prompts to frontier work. A different job from the doctrine. It teaches you, not your filing cabinet. This section, GitHub, or one 608-page book

The short version: build the layers with the guide. Climb the levels with the Curriculum. If your files are still chaos, start with the guide; the Curriculum assumes you have something for AI to work on. If your foundation stands and you want to get genuinely good, start climbing.

The five levels

Each level names where you are, where it takes you, and roughly how long the climb takes. Every level carries an Individual track and a Company track, and every technical section has a plain-language companion, so nobody gets locked out by jargon.

LevelYou areIt makes youTypical time
1 · EssentialsNew to AIA competent power user: the jargon, your own prompts, the right model for each task, real work shipped.30 days
2 · IntermediateComfortable with AIA builder: your own agents, RAG systems, MCP servers, evals, and cost control.60 to 90 days
3 · AdvancedA builderA systems designer: AI shipped to real users, incidents handled, drift caught, adversarial inputs survived.4 to 6 months
4 · ProfessionalA systems designerA practitioner: leading an AI team, custom training, applied alignment, safety engineering, red-teaming.6 to 12 months
5 · FrontierA practitionerA contributor to the field: original research, novel systems, open-weight training, foundation infrastructure.Ongoing

Which program first?

Start with the guide if:

  • Your files live in Downloads, Desktop, and email attachments
  • You explain the same preferences to AI every time you open a new chat
  • You have no rules for what AI may do without asking you
  • You want a Day One, a scorecard, and a finished system at the end

Start Day One

Start with the Curriculum if:

  • Your foundation stands, or you are here to learn, not to file
  • You know what a system prompt is and want to get past it
  • You want to build an agent, an MCP server, or a RAG pipeline
  • You want evals, not vibes

Start Level 1

Doing both, in sequence, is the whole arc: the guide gets the foundation right, the Curriculum builds the skill and the systems on top of it. The scorecard will tell you which side you should be standing on today.
