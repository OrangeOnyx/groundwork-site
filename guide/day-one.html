The Groundwork Guide / Day One
START HERE

Your first 30 minutes.

You do not need to understand the whole system to start. You need one sitting, one folder, and one honest page. Here is exactly what to do, minute by minute. No signup, no cost, nothing to install.

Minutes 0 to 3: find your starting point

Take the scorecard. Twenty-four quick questions, nothing leaves the page. It scores all six layers and names your weakest one, which is where your real work starts. Write that layer's name down.

Take the scorecard

Minutes 3 to 13: understand the machine

Read Foundations, at least the four building blocks at the top: the model is a well-read librarian, agents are its hands, prompts are instructions, and your documents are the fuel. Ten minutes, plain language. Every decision in the six layers makes sense once you have this picture.

Minutes 13 to 28: write your first document

Create one folder on your machine called Groundwork, and inside it a folder called 01_Identity. Open any text editor and start the first of the fifteen documents, the Identity Charter. A starter version:

## Who we are
[One clear paragraph. No marketing language.
Describe the actual operator or organization.]

Save it as 01_Identity/Identity_Charter.md. A rough draft counts. The "never become" list is the sharp edge: write at least one line that names something you have actually refused to do. Layer 1 has the full version and the standard for finishing it.

Minutes 28 to 30: set tomorrow up

Open the layer the scorecard named as your weakest and read its opening section. Stop there. That is day one, done: you know your gap, you understand the machine, and the first of fifteen documents exists on your machine, owned by you.

Where this goes: Phase 1 (Identity, Knowledge, Governance) is a focused weekend. Phase 2 activates one Operator in about a week. Phase 3 keeps it alive. The whole path is free and open in the guide, and the Field Guide PDF is the same doctrine in printable form.

Prefer a human in the room? The audit is free: sixty to ninety minutes with Adam, and you leave with a map of your gaps whether or not you go further.
