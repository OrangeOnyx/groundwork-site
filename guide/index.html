How this program runs

The program takes 90 days. In that time you build five layers, and you build them strictly in order. Each layer depends on the one below it. Layer 1 organizes your information. Layer 2 makes you legible. Layer 3 turns your knowledge into a vault an AI can reason over. Layer 4 automates routine work. Layer 5 puts rules around all of it. Skip a layer and everything above it wobbles.

Most people use AI backward. They ask it to perform without giving it context, get a generic answer, and conclude the tool is not ready for serious work. The tool was fine. It was uninformed. The fix is not a better prompt. The fix is a better foundation. That is what these 90 days build.

The rule: do not automate chaos. If your information is scattered, your identity is unclear, and your knowledge is not structured, automation will amplify the mess, not fix it. That is why automation is Layer 4, not Layer 1.

Why start now

Three pressures make this a now problem, not a someday problem.

Pressure 1

Prices will rise

Every major AI company is losing money. A $20 a month subscription costs the provider $60 to $200 in compute. Those subsidies end within about 18 months. Expect prices three to five times higher than today. The cheapest time to build is now.

Pressure 2

The advantage compounds

A personal intelligence system gets smarter every day you feed it. The person who starts today is 365 days ahead of the person who starts next year, and that gap never closes. It widens.

Pressure 3

It will be expected

Within two to three years, having a personal intelligence system will be as normal as having a smartphone. The question will stop being "do you use AI?" It will be "how well does your AI know your business?"

The people who build now will compound. Their AI gets smarter every week, so by the time prices rise, the value far exceeds the cost. The people who wait face higher prices, more complexity, and a gap that widens every month. At some point, catching up stops making economic sense.

What you have at day 90

Not theory. Not another course. A working system on your machine that compounds daily.

  • Every document findable in seconds. Digitized, named consistently, and organized so AI (and you) can find anything from the last 20 years instantly.
  • AI that actually knows your business. Ask a question and get an answer that references your specific documents, relationships, and history, not generic internet content.
  • Platform independence. Your vault works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any future AI. Markdown files on your machine. No lock-in. Ever.
  • Clear governance rules. You know exactly what AI can access, what it can do, and how to catch mistakes. Trust through boundaries.
  • 3 to 5 tasks automated safely. Routine work handled with clear approval rules. Human oversight where it matters. Time reclaimed where it does not.
  • Digital estate continuity. If something happens to you, your family or partners can access, understand, and maintain the system.
