The Groundwork Guide / Scorecard
WHERE DO YOU STAND

The AI Readiness Scorecard.

The same diagnostic Adam uses in audit sessions: thirty-five questions across the five layers.

How the scoring works

Thirty-five questions, seven per layer. Eight to ten minutes. Answer honestly. The value is in knowing exactly where you stand, not in getting a high score.

Every question is weighted. Standard questions count one point. High impact questions count two and carry a tag. Yes earns the full weight. Partial earns half, rounded up. No earns zero. Fifty-four points total.

Open the note under any question to see why it matters and what the fix is. When you finish, the results show your overall percentage, a layer-by-layer breakdown, your top priorities, and the path that fits.

Layer 1: Data Readiness

Seven questions on whether your information is digitized, findable, and clean. The fixes live in Layer 1.

1. Are your important business/personal documents digitized (not only on paper)? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. AI cannot read paper. Every document that exists only in physical form is invisible to your system.

The fix. Start with your highest-value documents: contracts, financials, insurance. Scan at 300+ DPI with OCR enabled.

2. Do your digital files follow a consistent naming convention? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Consistent names let AI find documents by pattern. 2026-08-01_Lease_AcmeCorp_Executed.pdf tells AI everything. scan_003.pdf tells it nothing.

The fix. Adopt the format: DATE_DocType_Entity_Status. Apply to new files immediately; backfill priority documents over 2 weeks.

3. Could you find a specific document from 3 years ago in under 60 seconds? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. If you can't find it, AI can't either. Retrieval speed is the test of whether your system actually works.

The fix. Your folder structure needs work. Layer 1 provides industry-specific templates that solve this.

4. Are your files stored in a searchable location (cloud drive, indexed local drive)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Files on an unindexed external drive or buried in email attachments are functionally lost to AI.

The fix. Consolidate to one primary location: Google Drive, OneDrive, or a local indexed folder. Cloud sync provides backup + accessibility.

5. Do you have a single source of truth for key business information (not duplicates in 5 places)? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Duplicates create contradictions. AI doesn't know which version is current. This causes wrong answers.

The fix. Designate one location as canonical. Archive or delete duplicates. Use version control (date in filename) for evolving documents.

6. Are your scanned documents OCR-processed (text is selectable, not just an image)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. A scan without OCR is just a picture. AI can't read pictures of text. It needs actual text data.

The fix. Re-process existing scans through an OCR tool (Adobe Acrobat, ABBYY FineReader, or a free OCR tool).

7. Do you have a regular process for filing new documents (not a pile-up system)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. A system that works once but isn't maintained degrades immediately. Filing must be habitual, not heroic.

The fix. Set a daily 5-minute filing habit. Or use automation: cloud folders with auto-naming rules.

Layer 2: Digital Identity

Seven questions on how you show up to AI answer engines. The fixes live in Layer 2.

8. Could a stranger understand exactly what you do within 10 seconds of visiting your website? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. AI answer engines read your website to decide whether to recommend you. If it's unclear to a human, it's unclear to AI.

The fix. Rewrite your homepage headline: [What you do] for [who you serve] in [where you operate]. Specific, not clever.

9. Is your LinkedIn profile consistent with your website (same services, same language)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Inconsistency confuses AI. If your website says 'property management' but LinkedIn says 'real estate consulting,' AI can't connect them.

The fix. Audit both. Pick one set of service descriptions and use them everywhere. Exact same language.

10. Do you have a documented brand voice (how you sound in writing)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Without a voice document, AI defaults to generic corporate tone. With one, everything it writes sounds like you.

The fix. Complete the Brand Voice builder in Layer 2. Takes 20 minutes. Transforms every AI-generated communication.

11. Could an AI accurately describe your business if asked 'Who is [your name] and what do they do?' High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. This is the GEO/AEO test. If AI can't answer this correctly today, you're invisible to AI-powered search.

The fix. Test it now: ask Claude or ChatGPT. Document what's wrong. The SEO/GEO/AEO section in Layer 2 shows you how to fix it.

12. Do you have an AI Brand Context File (a single document that tells AI everything about you)? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. This is the single most impactful document you can create. It turns generic AI into YOUR AI in one upload.

The fix. Build it in Layer 2's AI Context File section. 30 minutes. Immediate transformation in AI output quality.

13. Are your service offerings described specifically (not vaguely) across all platforms?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Vague descriptions ('We help businesses grow') are useless to AI. Specific descriptions ('Commercial lease negotiation for shopping centers in Lafayette, LA') are indexable.

The fix. Rewrite every service description to include: what it is, who it's for, where you do it, and what the outcome is.

14. Do you have a Google Business Profile with accurate, current information?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Google Business Profile is a primary data source for AI answer engines. Missing or outdated = invisible.

The fix. Create or update your GBP. Even if you're not a storefront, it helps AI understand your business.

Layer 3: Personal Intelligence Layer

Seven questions on your knowledge vault and its connection to an LLM. The fixes live in Layer 3.

15. Do you have a structured knowledge vault (Obsidian, Notion, or equivalent), not just scattered notes? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Scattered notes across 5 apps = no system. A vault is one place where all knowledge lives, connected and searchable.

The fix. Set up Obsidian (free, local-first). Layer 3's Vault Setup page walks you through the complete structure in 30 minutes.

16. Have you mapped your key entities (people, organizations, properties, accounts) into individual files? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Entity files let AI answer relationship questions: 'What's my history with Acme Corp?' requires an Acme Corp entity file.

The fix. Start with your top 20 entities. One file each. Name, type, relationship, key facts. Layer 3's Entity Mapping tool generates the structure.

17. Can your AI distinguish between your final documents and drafts? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Without authority levels, AI treats a draft contract the same as an executed one. This causes dangerous errors.

The fix. Add authority frontmatter (authoritative/draft/historical/private) to your vault files. Layer 3's Authority Classification page explains the system.

18. Have you connected your vault to at least one LLM (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.)? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. A vault without an LLM connection is a library without a librarian. The connection is what makes it useful.

The fix. Choose one LLM and connect it. Layer 3's LLM Connection page gives step-by-step for 5 different platforms.

19. Have you documented your preferences so AI doesn't have to ask the same questions repeatedly?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Every time AI asks 'what format do you prefer?', that's a failure of your system. Preferences should be documented once.

The fix. Create a preferences file in 03-Knowledge/preferences/. Include: communication style, formatting preferences, decision criteria, recurring instructions.

20. Does your AI give answers that reference YOUR specific information (not generic internet knowledge)? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. This is the ultimate test. If AI still gives generic answers, your vault isn't connected or isn't comprehensive enough.

The fix. Run the 5 verification tests in Layer 3's LLM Connection page. Identify which test fails and fix the underlying gap.

21. Could you switch AI providers (Claude → ChatGPT or vice versa) without losing any knowledge?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Platform independence is non-negotiable. If your knowledge only exists inside one AI's system, you don't own it.

The fix. Ensure all knowledge is in Markdown files you control locally. Never store critical information only in a chat history or proprietary format.

Layer 4: Workflow Automation

Seven questions on what runs without you. The fixes live in Layer 4.

22. Have you identified which of your routine tasks are repeatable and rule-based?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. You can't automate what you haven't mapped. The first step is knowing what you repeat.

The fix. Complete the Opportunity Audit in Layer 4. List every routine task, its frequency, time cost, and risk level.

23. Do you have clear rules for what AI can do without asking vs. what requires your approval? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Without an approval framework, you either over-trust AI (dangerous) or under-use it (wasteful). Clear rules solve both.

The fix. Build your Approval Framework in Layer 4. Three categories: Auto-Execute, Draft & Wait, Never Automate.

24. Do you have at least one workflow fully automated with AI handling it reliably? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. One working automation proves the concept and builds confidence. Zero automations means you're still doing everything manually.

The fix. Pick your highest-time, lowest-risk task from the Opportunity Audit. Automate it this week using Layer 4's First Sprint guide.

25. Can you measure how much time your automations save you per week?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. If you can't measure it, you can't prove value, to yourself or anyone else. Measurement also reveals which automations to expand.

The fix. Track time for 1 week: how long did the automated task take before? How long does review take now? The difference is your ROI.

26. Do your automations have error handling (what happens when AI gets it wrong)? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Every automation will eventually fail. Without error handling, failures cascade silently. With it, they're caught and corrected.

The fix. For each automation: define what 'wrong' looks like, what triggers a pause, and how corrections are logged.

27. Have you expanded beyond your first automation to at least 3 automated workflows?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. One automation is a proof of concept. Three is a system. The compound value comes from multiple workflows running simultaneously.

The fix. Use your Opportunity Audit rankings. Implement #2 and #3 using the same process that worked for #1.

28. Are your agent instructions documented (not just in your head or chat history)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Instructions in chat history disappear. Documented instructions in your vault persist, can be refined, and can be handed to any AI.

The fix. Move all working prompts/instructions into 05-Automations/agent-instructions/ as .md files. One file per workflow.

Layer 5: AI Ops & Governance

Seven questions on boundaries, verification, and continuity. The fixes live in Layer 5.

29. Have you defined what information AI can access freely vs. what's restricted? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Without access boundaries, AI might include private medical info in a business email or share confidential deal terms.

The fix. Complete the Access Boundaries builder in Layer 5. Categorize every type of information: full access, read-only, permission-required, never.

30. Do you run monthly verification checks on your AI system's accuracy? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Information decays. Contracts expire. People change roles. Without verification, AI gives increasingly stale answers over time.

The fix. Set a monthly calendar reminder. Run the 5 spot-checks in Layer 5's Verification Protocol. Log results. Fix drift.

31. Do you have a correction log (tracking when AI got something wrong and what you fixed)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Patterns in errors reveal systemic gaps. A correction log turns individual mistakes into system improvements.

The fix. Create 06-Maintenance/correction-log.md. Format: date, what went wrong, root cause, what was fixed. Review monthly.

32. Is there someone besides you who could access and maintain your system if needed? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. If you're the only person who knows how the system works, it dies with your availability. This is a business continuity risk.

The fix. Complete the Digital Estate plan in Layer 5. Designate a successor. Document access. Store securely outside the vault.

33. Do you have a process for ingesting new information (not just a growing pile of unfiled items)?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. A system that only works for existing information but can't handle new inputs is already decaying.

The fix. Create an ingestion queue (06-Maintenance/ingestion-queue.md). New items go here first, then get processed into the proper location weekly.

34. Could you switch AI providers today without losing any functionality?
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Platform independence is your insurance policy. If your provider raises prices 5x or shuts down, you need to move without pain.

The fix. Verify: all knowledge is in local Markdown files. All instructions are documented. No critical data exists only in one provider's system.

35. Do you have documented governance rules that your AI references before acting? High impact
Why it matters, and the fix

Why it matters. Governance rules are the 'operating agreement' for your AI. Without them, AI makes assumptions. With them, it follows your standards.

The fix. Create 00-System/governance-rules.md. Include: authority levels, access boundaries, approval framework, escalation rules, and banned actions.
